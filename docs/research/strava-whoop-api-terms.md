## 5. Does anything rule out the feature?

- **Showing an athlete their own Strava activity is allowed.** Agreement highlights and Policy §2.3: "Strava Data provided by a specific Strava user may be displayed or disclosed in your Developer Application only to that user." **C** BH Cycling shows each athlete only their own data, so the core use case passes. **I**
- **Showing it to anyone else (coach, team, other athletes) is banned**, even if the data is public on Strava (§2.3, §6.1, §6.2). **C** Any future coach-view feature would have to exclude Strava data. **I**
- **The open risks are §5.4 (no combining) and §5.2 (no supporting competitors).** Pairing a Strava activity with an intervals.icu Workout and marking it completed could be read as combining Strava Data with other data. intervals.icu is an activity-analysis platform that Strava told in 2024 was "in conflict with the updated terms". **I** Neither clause clearly forbids our case, and Strava alone decides compliance (§5 preamble). Agreement §2.2 invites questions to `developers@strava.com`. **C** Get a written answer before building on Strava.
- **The Strava subscription conflicts with the "prefer free" rule on map #181.** The developer must pay, and possibly end users too. **I**
- **The data layer must be built around the 7-day cache.** It can't store Strava activities in Firestore indefinitely. It needs a transient cache, re-fetching, and purging within 48 h of deletes. **C** (rules) / **I** (design impact)
- **No Strava data in AI tools, including dev tooling.** Use synthetic fixtures. Don't paste real Strava responses into Claude or other agents (§5.3, "context window"). **I** (applied to our workflow)
- **WHOOP has no equivalent blocker.** It is free, allows 10 members without review, imposes no anti-combining or competitor-platform clause, and only needs explicit opt-in to show data to others. Its caching follows the response cache header, with no permanent copies. **C** (rules) / **I** (conclusion)

## What this means for shipping

1. **Strava:** a working build for 1 athlete (up to 10 after self-upgrade) needs a paid Strava subscription. Going public needs a review with no SLA and no guarantee. Before committing, send `developers@strava.com` the specific use case (own-data display, pairing with intervals.icu Workouts) and get a written OK on §5.2 and §5.4.
2. **WHOOP** is the lower-risk second source under the terms. It needs a token-exchange backend (the existing `functions/`), a `DELETE /v2/user/access` call on disconnect and Account Deletion, and failed-refresh detection in place of a deauth webhook. A brand review of the PDF is still needed.
3. **Either way**, Account Deletion and disconnect must revoke the token and purge any cached data. The privacy policy needs the Strava §2.1 disclosures, and a written deletion confirmation is required for Strava (§2.5). For Strava we would also need a webhook endpoint for deauth and deletes, answering within 2 s.
